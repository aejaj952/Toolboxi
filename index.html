How the age calculator works

Age is the time between your date of birth and today. The calculator subtracts the two dates, borrowing days from the previous month and months from the year where needed, so the result matches how people count age in everyday life. It also converts the same span into total days, hours and minutes.

When an exact age is useful

  • Checking minimum-age rules for exams, jobs, driving licences and government forms.
  • Filling school or college admission forms that ask for age on a particular date.
  • Finding your age in years, months and days for medical or insurance paperwork.
  • Planning birthdays and anniversaries with a countdown to the next one.

Leap years are handled automatically because the tool relies on your device's calendar. Your date of birth is processed only in your browser and is never stored.

What is a QR code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a square barcode that stores text or a web address. Point a phone camera at it and the content opens in seconds. The codes made here are static, so they never expire and need no account.

Ways to use your QR code

  • Link a shop, restaurant menu or social media profile on a poster or visiting card.
  • Share a Google Form, portfolio, YouTube video or WhatsApp chat link.
  • Add contact details, event information or a short message to printed material.

Tips for a code that scans well

Keep the content short, print the code at least 2 cm wide, and leave a blank margin around it. Dark code on a light background scans most reliably. Always test it with your phone before printing in bulk.
