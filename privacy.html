The short version

  • Your files stay on your device. The tools process PDFs, images, text and passwords in your browser and do not upload them to a ToolBoxi server.
  • No accounts, no sign-up. We do not ask for your name or payment details.
  • Standard web data still exists. Hosting, CDN and font providers see your IP address and browser details when they deliver the site.
  • Ads and affiliate links. Google AdSense may show ads using cookies, and some links may earn us a commission at no cost to you. You can control personalization.

Who we are

ToolBoxi (“ToolBoxi”, “we”, “us”) is a free website at toolboxi.online that offers browser-based utilities, including PDF tools (merge, split, compress, image to PDF), ZIP tools, an age calculator, a QR code generator and a password strength checker and generator. The site is run by an independent individual operator based in India.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use the site, how cookies and advertising work, and the choices you have. By using ToolBoxi you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the site.

Contact for privacy questions: support@toolboxi.online

Your files and inputs stay in your browser

ToolBoxi is built so that its tools run locally in your web browser, using JavaScript on your own device. When you use a tool:

  • The PDFs, images, ZIP contents, text, dates of birth, links and passwords you select or type are processed in your browser’s memory.
  • By default, they are not uploaded to a ToolBoxi server. ToolBoxi does not operate a backend that receives, stores or reviews your files or inputs.
  • Results (for example a merged PDF or a QR code image) are created on your device and downloaded or copied by your browser.
  • Closing or refreshing the tab clears what was held in memory. We do not keep copies.
Passwords: the password checker and generator also run in your browser and nothing you type is sent to us. As a general habit, though, test a similar password rather than a real one on any website.

“Not uploaded” describes what the ToolBoxi tools do. It does not cover things outside our control, such as browser extensions, malware, shared or monitored devices, or your operating system’s own file handling. Please keep your device and browser up to date.

Information we and our providers may receive

Even though your files stay on your device, loading any website involves some technical data. Here is an honest breakdown.

Technical data from your visit

When your browser requests a page, the hosting and network providers behind the site (see the next section) necessarily receive standard request data such as your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, the pages requested and the time of the request. This data may appear in their server or security logs. ToolBoxi does not have a database of visitors and does not combine this data with your files or inputs.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply, and we keep the message for as long as needed to handle your request and for ordinary record keeping.

Advertising and analytics data

If advertising or analytics are active on the site, the providers involved may collect cookie identifiers, device information and usage information as described in the sections on analytics and advertising below.

What we do not ask for

ToolBoxi has no accounts, logins or sign-up, and we do not ask for your name, address, phone number or payment details. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information.

Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature that keeps small pieces of data on your device. Here is how ToolBoxi uses them.

TypePurposeSet by
Preference (local storage)Remembers your light or dark mode choice (a single value). It stays on your device and is never sent to us.ToolBoxi
Analytics (if enabled)Helps us understand which pages and tools are used so we can improve the site. See the next section.The analytics provider
AdvertisingUsed to show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse. See the advertising section.Google and its ad partners

ToolBoxi itself does not set tracking cookies. You can block or delete cookies and clear local storage in your browser settings at any time. Blocking some of them may mean ads are less relevant or that your theme preference is not remembered, but the tools will still work.

Analytics and Google Search Console

Search Console. We may use Google Search Console to see how the site appears in Google Search, for example which searches lead to the site and whether pages are indexed correctly. It reports aggregated search performance data to us and does not place a tracking cookie on your device through this site.

Site analytics. We may use a privacy-focused analytics service to count visits and page views. If one is switched on, it will measure things like pages viewed, approximate country, device type and referrer, and we will update this policy with the provider’s name. Analytics never include the content of your files or the text you enter into a tool.

Google Analytics. At the time of writing ToolBoxi does not rely on Google Analytics. If we add it, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

ToolBoxi is free to use and is supported by advertising, which may be shown through Google AdSense. Ads appear in clearly marked areas of the page, never inside a tool’s controls. The following applies when ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to ToolBoxi and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Ads may be personalized (based on your interests and earlier activity) or non-personalized (based mainly on the page you are viewing and your general location). Non-personalized ads may still use cookies for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • We do not give advertisers or Google any files, text or inputs you process in the tools, and we do not control the data Google collects through its own cookies.

Your advertising choices

  • Manage or turn off ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and how its ad technologies work at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Opt out of interest-based ads from many other vendors at aboutads.info/choices (US), youronlinechoices.com (Europe) and networkadvertising.org.
  • Use your browser or device controls, such as blocking third-party cookies or resetting your mobile advertising ID.

Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the law and Google’s policies require your consent before cookies are used for personalized advertising. Where this applies, ToolBoxi will present a consent message that lets you accept, reject or manage these choices, and you can change your decision later through the same control.

Affiliate links

Some links or recommendations on ToolBoxi may be affiliate links. If you click one and then buy a product or sign up for a service, ToolBoxi may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links do not change the price you pay.

The partner site you visit may set its own cookies to record that you came from ToolBoxi, which is how the commission is tracked. That partner’s own privacy policy governs what it collects. We aim to label affiliate links clearly, and the tools themselves stay free and work the same whether or not you use any link.

How we use information

The limited information that reaches us or our service providers is used to:

  • deliver, secure and keep the website running, and diagnose errors and abuse;
  • understand how the site is used so we can improve tools and fix problems;
  • show and measure advertising and track affiliate referrals;
  • reply to messages you send us; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not use your files or inputs for advertising, profiling or training of any kind. Because ad partners may use cookies to show interest-based ads, some privacy laws treat that as “sharing” or “targeted advertising”. You can opt out using the choices in the advertising section.

Legal bases and sharing

Where laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on: consent for advertising cookies and any non-essential analytics; legitimate interests for keeping the site secure and reliable and responding to your messages; and legal obligation where we must keep or disclose something by law.

We share information only with: the hosting, network and CDN providers that deliver the site; advertising and analytics providers described above; our email provider if you contact us; and authorities when legally required. These providers act under their own terms and privacy policies.

How long data is kept

Files and inputs processed in the tools are not kept by us; they exist in your browser until you close or refresh the page. Your theme preference stays in your browser until you clear it. Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to handle your request. Logs, cookies and ad data held by third-party providers are kept under their own retention policies.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time, to receive a copy of your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. This includes rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Because ToolBoxi has no accounts and does not keep a visitor database, there is usually very little we can look up. To exercise a right, or to ask about the few things we might hold (such as your emails to us), write to support@toolboxi.online. For data held by Google or other third parties, use their own tools, such as the ad settings linked above.

We honour browser opt-out signals such as Global Privacy Control where the law requires. Our own code does not track you across sites, so “Do Not Track” has no extra effect on the tools, but third-party ad technologies may respond to it differently.

Children’s privacy

ToolBoxi is a general-audience website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Parents and guardians can also limit ad personalization through Google’s settings and their device controls.

International transfers and security

The providers named in this policy operate globally, so technical data about your visit may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Those providers use their own safeguards for such transfers.

The site is served over HTTPS, and keeping files on your device means they are not exposed to a ToolBoxi server. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to other sites

ToolBoxi may link to external websites, including advertisers and affiliate partners. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their policies before sharing any information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the site, its features, or the law changes, for example if we turn on a new analytics or advertising service. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest revision. Continuing to use ToolBoxi after an update means you accept the revised policy, and where the law requires fresh consent, we will ask for it.
